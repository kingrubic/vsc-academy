FAQ

QUESTIONS
PEOPLE ASK FIRST

Short answers before you choose a program and a class date.

Do I need programming experience?+

No. Most programs at VSC Academy are built for practical use, so you can work with AI effectively without a programming background. In more advanced sessions, the instructor guides each step so you can still practise.

Which program should I start with?+

If you are new to AI, start with AI Starter. If you already use AI and want a more structured way of working, choose Applied AI for Work. If you already use AI regularly and want to build a workflow, automation or an AI Agent, choose AI Agent & Automation.

I am new to AI. Can I still join?+

Yes. Programs are organised by level, from a first applied experience through to more advanced work. If you are starting out, begin with the introductory program before moving on.

Is the teaching mostly theory or practice?+

Programs at VSC Academy prioritise practice and building. You get enough foundation to understand the problem, then work on a real task, a workflow, a system or an AI Agent you can keep using.

What equipment do I need?+

A laptop and a stable internet connection are enough for learning and practice. Some programs or workshops may ask you to prepare a tool or account in advance so the session can run smoothly.

Is there support after the program?+

Yes. Depending on the program, you receive materials, practice guidance or support for a defined period so you can continue refining what you built.

Are there online classes?+

VSC Academy runs both in-person and live online classes, depending on the program. Format is listed clearly on each course page and on the schedule.

Can I request an invoice?+

Yes. VSC Academy can issue an invoice for a learner or an organisation. Provide the required details during registration or when confirming payment.

What if I am not sure which program is right?+

Contact VSC Academy with your current work and goal. We will help you choose a program rather than asking you to guess.
