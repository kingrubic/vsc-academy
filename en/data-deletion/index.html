USER DATA DELETION

USER DATA
DELETION INSTRUCTIONS

This page explains how to ask VSC Academy to delete your personal data, including website records, course registration, the learner portal, and any data received through a Meta / Facebook app.

Updated: 21 August 2026 Applies to vscacademy.vn

CONTENTS

01 · How to request deletion 02 · Meta / Facebook data 03 · What we delete 04 · What we may keep 05 · Timeline 06 · Contact
01

How to request deletion of your data

  1. Email vscacademy8@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request
  3. Include your full name, phone number, the email you used with VSC Academy, and if relevant: your Facebook / Instagram name or App-scoped User ID
  4. Say whether you want all of your data deleted, or only data received from Meta / Facebook

We may ask you to confirm your identity before we delete the records. Do not send passwords or one-time codes in the email.

02

If you used Facebook, Instagram or a Meta app

In addition to emailing VSC Academy, remove the app on Meta so no new Facebook data is shared:

  1. Open Facebook in a browser and sign in
  2. Go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites
  3. Find the VSC Academy app (or the matching Meta app name) and choose Remove
  4. If Facebook asks, tick the option to request deletion of data the app received from Facebook

Removing the app on Facebook does not automatically delete a course registration stored by VSC Academy. Email us using step 01 if you also want VSC Academy records deleted.

03

What we delete

  • Contact and registration form details
  • Learner-portal accounts and login sessions
  • Consent to receive programme updates
  • Any data received about you from Meta / Facebook
  • Form drafts stored on our side, if any remain
04

What we may have to keep

Some payment, invoice, issued-certificate or accounting / tax records may be kept at a minimum level required by Vietnamese law, even if we delete your learner profile.

05

How long it takes

VSC Academy will confirm we received the request and complete deletion or reply within 30 days. If the law requires more time, we will tell you within that period.
