VSC ACADEMY

HOW WE HANDLE
YOUR INFORMATION

This page explains how VSC Academy collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the website, register for a programme, contact us or sign in to the learner portal.

Updated: 21 August 2026 Applies to vscacademy.vn

CONTENTS

01

Who is responsible for your data

VSC Academy — a member of the VSC Group ecosystem — is the controller of personal data for the activities on this website.

In-person classes currently take place at The Comma Coffee, 21 Hoa Mai, Cau Kieu Ward, Ho Chi Minh City. Please send any privacy request to the email or phone numbers in the Contact section.

02

When this policy applies

This policy applies when you:

  • Visit the VSC Academy website
  • Submit a contact or registration form
  • Message, call or write to us on Zalo, email or phone
  • Use the learner portal or instructor portal
  • Pay tuition by bank transfer
  • Receive a certificate that other people can verify

If you do not provide the required fields on a form, we cannot take your request, confirm a place on a class, or support you after registration.

03

What information we collect

When you contact us: full name, phone or Zalo number, email, the topic you need help with, and your message.

When you register: full name, phone, email, current role, organisation (if given), current AI experience, learning goal, chosen programme and class, how you heard about VSC Academy, a referral code or name (if given), agreement to this privacy policy, and an optional choice to receive updates.

When you need an invoice: organisation name, tax code, invoice email and organisation address.

When you study with us: learner-portal account details, enrolment status, attendance, progress, certificates, and login history needed to run the class.

When you pay by transfer: information that appears on the bank transaction (payer name, amount, transfer content). The website does not collect payment-card numbers.

We do not actively collect sensitive data such as health information, biometrics or social-media account credentials, unless you include them yourself in a message.

04

How we use the information

  • Receive and reply to enquiries
  • Process registration, confirm a class place and send payment guidance
  • Run the class, take attendance, support learners and issue certificates
  • Issue an invoice when you ask for one
  • Send programme or workshop updates — only if you opt in separately
  • Operate the website, protect accounts and handle technical issues
  • Meet accounting, tax and legal duties where they apply

We do not sell personal data. Marketing messages are sent only if you tick the optional updates box; you can withdraw that consent at any time.

05

How we process the information

Data comes in through website forms, direct messages, or records created by the team when a registration or learner account is set up. It is stored in VSC Academy’s administration system so the team can run classes and support you.

Confirmation, account-activation and learner-support emails may be sent through the email service VSC Academy uses. Learner-portal passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text.

06

Who may receive the information

We share data only when it is needed for the purposes above, including:

  • VSC Academy staff who handle advice, class operations, accounting and learner support
  • The bank that receives tuition — only the details that appear on the transfer
  • Technical or email providers, limited to what they need to run the service
  • Public authorities when the law requires it

In-person classes take place at a partner venue. We do not share the learner list with the venue unless that is needed for access to the class and you have been told.

07

Cookies and browser storage

The website uses a small amount of technical data:

  • Session cookies for the admin, learner and instructor portals — to keep you signed in, not for advertising
  • Your VI/EN language choice, stored in the browser
  • A draft of the registration or contact form, so you do not lose what you typed if you reload the page

The website does not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel or similar advertising tools. If measurement tools are added later, this page will be updated.

08

How long we keep the information

  • Contact requests: for as long as we need to handle and follow up the request
  • Registration, payment and invoice records: for accounting, tax and complaint-handling periods
  • Learner, attendance and certificate records: for as long as we need to run the class, verify certificates and support you afterwards
  • Learner-portal accounts: until the account is closed on request or is no longer in use

When we no longer have a valid reason to keep data, we delete it, anonymise it, or retain only what the law requires.

09

How we protect the information

VSC Academy limits access by role, uses protected login sessions, hashes passwords and stores records on internal systems. No system is perfectly secure. If an incident affects your data, we will handle it and notify you as required.

10

Your rights

Under Vietnamese personal-data protection law, you may:

  • Know what data is being processed
  • See, correct or complete information that is wrong or missing
  • Withdraw consent to marketing updates
  • Ask us to delete data when there is no longer a basis to keep it, except where the law requires retention. See the user data deletion instructions
  • Raise a complaint if you believe your data is being used for the wrong purpose

Send a request to vscacademy8@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity first. Some registration, payment, invoice or certificate records may have to be kept for legal reasons.

11

Names on certificates can be verified in public

When VSC Academy issues a certificate, anyone with the Certificate ID can look it up on the verification page. A valid result shows the learner’s name, programme name, completion date, issue date and certificate code.

This helps an employer or partner check that a certificate is genuine. If you do not want your name shown on the verification page, contact us before the certificate is issued.

12

When this policy changes

We may update this page when our collection or use of data changes. The update date is at the top of the page. For a material change, VSC Academy will announce it on the website or by email where that is practical.
